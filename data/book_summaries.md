## Title: 1984
A dystopian society where Big Brother watches all.
Winston Smith yearns for truth and freedom amid surveillance and propaganda.
Themes: totalitarianism, surveillance, truth vs. propaganda, freedom.

## Title: Brave New World
A future engineered for pleasure and stability; individuality traded for comfort.
Themes: hedonism, social control, technology, conformity.

## Title: The Hobbit
Reluctant Bilbo joins dwarves to reclaim treasure from Smaug and finds courage.
Themes: adventure, friendship, courage, home.

## Title: The Lord of the Rings
A fellowship strives to destroy the One Ring and resist corrupting power.
Themes: good vs. evil, fellowship, sacrifice, power.

## Title: Dune
On Arrakis, Paul faces politics, prophecy, and ecology shaping destiny.
Themes: destiny, ecology, politics, religion.

## Title: The Name of the Wind
Kvothe tells how he became a legend through grit, talent, and loss.
Themes: storytelling, identity, magic, loss.

## Title: The Book Thief
During WWII, Liesel steals books and shares stories that heal.
Themes: war, friendship, words, loss.

## Title: All Quiet on the Western Front
Young soldiers meet the brutal truth of war and shattered ideals.
Themes: war, trauma, disillusionment, friendship.

## Title: The Old Man and the Sea
Santiago’s epic struggle with a marlin tests pride and perseverance.
Themes: perseverance, pride, nature, isolation.

## Title: The Road
A father and son carry the “fire” through a ruined world.
Themes: survival, parenthood, hope, morality.

## Title: The Left Hand of Darkness
On icy Gethen, an envoy learns to see beyond gender and politics.
Themes: gender, politics, communication, friendship.

## Title: Ender's Game
A child commander is forged by games for an existential war.
Themes: strategy, childhood, ethics, war.

## Title: The Night Circus
A nocturnal circus hosts a magical duel entwined with love.
Themes: love, rivalry, magic, fate.

## Title: Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone
A boy wizard finds friends, identity, and courage at Hogwarts.
Themes: friendship, magic, identity, courage.
